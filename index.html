Research

Where are predictions hardest on a sphere?

MSc dissertation: Adaptive Geodesic Conformal Prediction for Uncertainty-Quantified Spherical Harmonic Reconstruction of Climate Fields. It makes the uncertainty of a global temperature reconstruction honest in hard regions, not only on average.

3,510cities, July 2003 data
76.7% → 94.8%coverage in the hardest regions (target 90%)
r = 0.634local difficulty vs true error
L = 8 to 30harmonic degrees tested
  • Extended a spherical harmonic reconstruction of global land temperature with adaptive geodesic conformal prediction, so interval widths follow local difficulty.
  • Standard conformal prediction looked fine overall (90.9% coverage) but fell to 76.7% in the hardest regions. The adaptive method kept 94.8% there at a similar mean width (4.59 °C against 4.65 °C).
  • Estimated local difficulty with geodesic k-nearest neighbours, and tested robustness across degrees and two λ-selection criteria.
  • 17 of the 20 highest-uncertainty cities are in the high-altitude Andes (Bolivia, Peru and northern Chile).

Builds on the spherical harmonic reconstruction by Will Thomas (2025).
